# Focus Management

## 1. WWhat is Deep Work?

- High-focus, high-value work without distractions.
- Produces better results and helps develop skills.
- Opposite of shallow work (emails, social media).


## 2. According to author how to do deep work properly, in a few points?

- Work Deeply: Schedule uninterrupted focus blocks.
- Embrace Boredom: Resist constant stimulation.
- Limit Social Media: Use only tools with meaningful benefits.
- Drain the Shallows: Minimize low-value tasks.
- Set Rituals & Goals: Define clear work routines.


## 3. How can you implement the principles in your day to day life?

- Schedule 90–120 min deep work sessions.
- Turn off notifications; avoid distractions.
- Break tasks into small steps to start easily.
- Take diffused mode breaks (walk, meditate, chores).
- Track progress to reinforce focus habits.


## 4. What are the dangers of social media, in brief?

- Reduces focus and fragments attention.
- Steals time from productive work.
- Encourages shallow interactions.

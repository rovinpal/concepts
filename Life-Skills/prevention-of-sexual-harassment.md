# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment includes any unwanted behaviour that makes a person feel uncomfortable, unsafe, embarrassed, or disrespected.

- Unwanted touching or physical contact
- Sexual comments, jokes, or messages
- Staring, gestures, or inappropriate looks
- Asking for sexual favours or repeated unwanted requests for dates
- Sharing inappropriate photos, videos, or content
- Bullying, teasing, or insulting someone based on gender or sexuality

## What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

- Stay calm and clearly tell the person to stop if possible
- Support the affected person and listen without blaming them
- Save any proof like messages, emails, or screenshots
- Report the incident to the manager, HR, or Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)
- Do not ignore repeated incidents or treat them as jokes
- Help create a safe and respectful workplace for everyone

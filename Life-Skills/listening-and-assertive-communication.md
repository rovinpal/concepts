# Listening and Assertive Communication

## What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

* Try not to zone out of conversation to your own thoughts
* Focus on the speaker and the topic 
* Do not interrupt when a person is speaking and wait for them to finish
* Use body language to show that you understand the speaker
* Respond verbally to let them know that you are listening (I see, Ok, Tell me more, Is it?, etc)
* Ask questions to clarify to ensure understanding
* Paraphrase the speaker's thoughts to show you understood what he/she said 

## According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

* Its a form of listening where you mirror speaker's body language and emotion
* Not only should you listen carefully to speaker's words but also the emotion behind them
* Reflect back speaker's body language and his/her feelings to show you understand them
* Paraphrase what speaker said to to show you understood them
* Focus on empathy and building trust

## What are the obstacles in your listening process?

* Being distracted by external noises, activities, texting, etc
* Being distracted by internal thoughts and zoning out of conversation
* Assuming speaker's thoughts and opinions and forming own conclusions
* Interrupting the speaker before trying to let them finish and understand their thought

## What can you do to improve your listening?

* Try not get distracted while the speaker is speaking
* Have patience and let the speaker finsh his/her thought before responding in any way 
* Take down notes if needed 
* Paraphrase what speaker said to show you understand them and that you are on the same page

## When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day to day life?

* When you stop expressing your opinions and needs
* When you are unsure and fear conflict
* When you are scared of confrontation
* When you dont want to hurt someone's feelings
* When you feel undervalued or think you will be ignored

## When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day to day life?

* When you feel frustrated and angry
* When you want to assert dominance

## When do you switch into Passive Aggressive (sarcasm/gossiping/taunts/silent treatment and others) communication styles in your day to day life?

* When you are upset but also dont want to confront anyone about it 
* When you stop expressing your feelings openly
* When you want to show frustration but you feel ignored or dismissed

## How can you make your communication assertive? You can watch and analyse the videos, then think what would be a few steps you can apply in your own life?

* Try to learn about your own self and your feelings
* Understand your needs
* Try to communicate with people of your feelings and needs in minor situations
* Make sure to have body languages and tones that does not come off as rude or disrespectful
* Be open about your feelings at every situations to ensure you are not being dismissed or undervalued

# Learning Process

## What is the Feynman Technique?

* Explaining a topic in simpler way to a someone who does not have the same base knowledge of the topic as you, so that they can understand it well.


## In the video of How to Learn TED talk by Barbara Oakley, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

* The idea of not getting worried about making mistakes rather beleive it helps in learning better from mistakes.
* The idea of not putting a lot of focus on a thought rather free your mind and switch to relax mode and look at thing on broader perspective.
* The brain builds stronger understanding skills with consistent practice.
* The importance of being curious and willingness to learn and understand.

  
## What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

### Active mode

* When you are highly focused on a topic which you are aware of.
* Helps in trying to solve problems due to previous knowledge.
 
### Diffuse mode

* When you are unable to understand the topic or idea and you free your mind.
* Helps look at the concepts in a broader perspective which helps you understand easily.


## According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?

* Break down skills into smaller parts.
* Learn enough to have knowledge to correct yourself.
* Remove distractions.
* Practice for atleast 20 hors. 


## What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?

* Practice explaining concepts and idea in simpler words.
* Minimize distraction by turning off unnecessary notification, spending less time on social media, etc.
* Focus deeply on one thought at a time.
* Practice consistently. 
* Communicate clearly when you face any issues.

# Grit and Growth Mindset

## Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H14bBuluwB8)

* Grit is basically combination of passion and commitment to certain goals.
* People who work hard, who learn and practice and work on improving consistently are more likely to succeed than those who rely on talent or knowledge.

## Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=75GFzikmRY0)

* People with growth mindset are more likely to achieve their target goals as compared to people with fixed mindset.
* We need change out beliefs and focus in order to have a growth mindset.
* People need to put effort, accept challenges, learn from mistakes and continue improving.

## What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?

* It means that believing that our effort, hard work, choices, and decision is what leads to our success rather than heavily relying on raw talent or luck.
* We must feel like we have control over our life to stay motivated.

## What are the key points mentioned by speaker to build growth mindset?

* Believe that you can figure things out.
* Question our assumptions that limit us.
* Develop our own life curriculum.
* Honor the struggle.

## What are your ideas to take action and build Growth Mindset?

* Practice coding regularly and stay consistent
* Treat mistakes and bugs as learning opportunities
* Ask questions and seek feedback from mentors and peers
* Maintain a positive attitude toward challenges
* Build discipline and continue learning every day

## How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P.

* You can make habits easier by making them simpler, having a clear reminder, and not needing too much motivation.
* MAP means
   - M = Motivation 
   - A = Ability
   - P = Prompt

## Why it is important to "Shine" or Celebrate after each successful completion of habit?

Celebrating after doing a habit makes your brain feel good about it. When you feel good, you are more likely to do the habit again.

## What is the book's perspective about Identity?

Habits are really about who you want to be, not just what you want to do. Its like if you see yourself as a healthy person, you are more likely to eat healthy or exercise.

## Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?

* Make it simple and easy.
* Start really small.
* Keep things you need nearby.
* Do it after something you already do.

## Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?

* Make it harder or take more effort.
* Hide or remove things that remind you.
* Add extra steps so it’s annoying.
* Make it less fun.
